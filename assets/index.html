Executive Summary

Technical Defense Meets Venture Acceleration

Mehdi Nacer Kerkar is a passionate Senior OT/IT Cybersecurity Specialist with over six years of experience delivering strategic advisory and technical defense across the EMEA region (PwC, EY, and independent engagements in Algeria, KSA, Qatar, and France).

Holding a Master's degree in Information System Security along with certifications (CISSP, CCISO, ISA/IEC 62443 CFS, PECB Senior Lead SCADA Security Manager), he acts with a CISO mindset. In addition to securing critical infrastructure, he serves as a FAST-structured Strategic Advisor for tech startups—helping founders build enterprise trust, clear complex vendor security audits, and unlock B2B sales pipelines.
